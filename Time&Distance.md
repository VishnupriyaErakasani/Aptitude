# Time and Distance 

---

## 1. Basic Formula (Foundation)

> **Distance = Speed × Time**

From this:

* **Speed = Distance / Time**
* **Time = Distance / Speed**

Always keep **units consistent**.

---

## 2. Unit Conversion Tricks

### Speed Conversion

* km/hr → m/s : multiply by **5/18**
* m/s → km/hr : multiply by **18/5**


---

## 3. Average Speed Tricks

### Case 1: Equal Distances

> Average Speed = **2ab / (a + b)**

Where:

* a = speed for first half
* b = speed for second half

📌 Very common trick question.

---

### Case 2: Equal Time

> Average Speed = **(a + b) / 2**

Simple arithmetic mean.

---

## 4. Relative Speed Concept (Very Important)

### Same Direction

> Relative Speed = |a − b|

### Opposite Direction

> Relative Speed = a + b

Used in:

* Trains
* Races
* Boats
* People walking

---

## 5. Meeting Point Trick

If two objects start from same point:

> Time to meet = Distance / (Sum of speeds)

If opposite directions → add speeds

If same direction → subtract speeds

---

## 6. Catch-Up (Chasing) Problems

When one starts later:

> Time to catch = (Initial distance gap) / (Relative speed)

📌 Faster − Slower speed

---

## 7. Train Problems (Most Scoring Area)

### Train Crossing a Pole / Man

> Time = Length of train / Speed

### Train Crossing Platform

> Time = (Train length + Platform length) / Speed

### Two Trains Crossing Each Other

> Time = (Sum of lengths) / (Relative speed)

---

## 8. Speed Ratio Trick

If speeds are in ratio **a : b**

> Time ratio = **b : a**

📌 Speed and time are inversely proportional.

---

## 9. Change in Speed – Percentage Trick

### Speed Increase by x%

> Time decreases by **x / (100 + x)**

### Speed Decrease by x%

> Time increases by **x / (100 − x)**

📌 Very high probability questions.

---

## 10. Boats and Streams

Let:

* u = speed of boat in still water
* v = speed of stream

### Downstream Speed

> u + v

### Upstream Speed

> u − v

### Boat Speed in Still Water

> (Downstream + Upstream) / 2

### Stream Speed

> (Downstream − Upstream) / 2

---

## 11. Circular Track Problems

### One Round Time

> Time = Circumference / Speed

### Meeting on Circular Track

* Same direction → use difference of speeds
* Opposite direction → use sum of speeds

---

## 12. Race Problems

If A beats B by x meters:

> A's speed / B's speed = Total distance / (Total distance − x)

If time is given, convert distance first.

---

## 13. Partial Distance – Fraction Trick

If a person covers:

* x part at speed a
* remaining at speed b

Use:

> Total time = (x/a) + ((1−x)/b)

---

## 14. Common Shortcuts to Remember

* Faster speed → less time
* Same distance → use harmonic mean
* Same time → use arithmetic mean
* Always convert km/hr to m/s in train problems

---

## 15. Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Mixing units
❌ Using average speed formula blindly
❌ Forgetting relative speed
❌ Ignoring start-time difference

---

## 16. One-Line Revision Sheet

* D = S × T
* Avg speed (equal dist) = 2ab/(a+b)
* Relative speed (opp) = a+b
* Relative speed (same) = |a−b|
* Train + platform = add lengths
* Boat downstream = u+v
* Speed ↑ → Time ↓

---
